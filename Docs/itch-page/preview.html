Joker cards

Take the reward.
Or take another chance.

Regular level-ups offer a choice of three cards. The Joker adds a second decision: keep the rewards you've lined up, or roll again for more.

Successful rolls can offer higher-tier cards and more choices. A failed roll clears pending rewards and can also take away upgrades you've already claimed.

The stronger your run becomes, the harder it is to walk away.

Beyond the next wave

Put your build to the test.

Enemy waves give way to boss encounters. Keep moving through their attack patterns while your weapons and drone keep firing.

Between runs, visit the lobby shop, manage your inventory, and equip items that affect your stats. Your card choices shape how each new run plays.

Getting started

Before you launch.

Running a Windows build

Extract the entire ZIP into a folder, then launch the game executable. Keep the accompanying data folder and supporting files together.

Do I choose one weapon?

You can add different weapon types through cards and combine them in the same run. The drone attacks independently alongside your ship.

When does the nuclear strike fire?

It activates when you claim its card. It is a special strike, rather than a weapon you fire repeatedly.
